- **A drag from a selection group's bare space selects its text.** A press in a group's margin or in
  the gap between its paragraphs cleared the selection, so a drag begun just beside the text selected
  nothing, where a web page starts selecting. Such a press now starts at the group's text nearest the
  point (the same line, else the nearest line) and goes on as a press on that text, so the drag
  extends it. A press on a control inside the group, or outside every group, is unchanged.
