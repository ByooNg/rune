- Scroll: a vertical wheel or trackpad gesture moves the innermost viewport under the pointer that can move its way, and keeps moving that one for the rest of the gesture and its momentum. A short nested viewport with nothing to scroll (a capped code block that fits) no longer takes every delta and stops the page around it. `Tx::keep_on_screen` keeps a wheel delta that landed since the last frame, so content loading above mid-swipe no longer swallows that frame of the swipe. A wheel scroll is timed as a `scroll` interaction on the viewport it moved. `RUNE_SCROLL_LOG=<file>` logs a keyed viewport's offset and rows each frame, and each wheel delta's viewport.
